# How SalesDriver Works

Outbound on SalesDriver follows a repeatable system from ICP to booked meeting.

## Steps

1. **Define ICP**: firmographics, triggers, and buyer roles
2. **Build lists**: data tools, verification, and enrichment in one credit pool
3. **Launch sequences**: email, LinkedIn, and multichannel cadences
4. **AI follow-up**: Cora and Prompt Vault assist replies and research
5. **Sync CRM**: activities, stages, and reporting stay connected
6. **Optimize**: benchmarks, templates, and managed services when you need hands-on help
